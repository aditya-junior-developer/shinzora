1. What we collect

Account data

  • name, email address and phone number;
  • password, stored only as a hash by our authentication provider — we never see it;
  • sign-in timestamps and session metadata;
  • if you use Google sign-in, the account identifier and email Google returns.

Business data you enter

  • business name, category, description, location, contact details, audience and goals;
  • projects, generated files, versions, prompts and design decisions;
  • products, prices, stock, orders and customer records you add to the store;
  • support tickets, service requests and campaign plans.

Payment data

  • the amount, purpose, reference and status of a payment;
  • the UPI transaction reference (UTR) you submit for verification;
  • records of who verified a payment and when.

We do not store card numbers, UPI PINs or bank credentials. Those are handled by your bank or payment app.

Usage data

  • AI generations used per billing cycle, and errors returned by the provider;
  • an activity log of actions in your workspace, used for support and security.

2. Why we collect it

  • To operate the product — generate a site from your brief, keep versions, publish, and show your own data back to you.
  • To bill correctly — match a payment to the right account and service.
  • To support you — understand what you were doing when something failed.
  • To keep the platform secure — detect abuse, credential stuffing and attempts to escalate privileges.
  • To meet legal obligations — keep records of payments and invoices where required.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your business content to train models ourselves.

3. AI processing

When you use the assistant, the builder or any AI action, the request is sent from our Edge Function to Google's Gemini API. The payload contains the instruction you wrote, the business context you saved and, for revisions, the current project files. Google processes it to return a response under its API terms.

API keys live only in server-side secrets. Conversations and generated versions are stored in your Supabase database so you can resume work; you can delete a conversation from the assistant, and deleting a project removes its versions.

Do not paste passwords, payment card details, Aadhaar numbers or other sensitive personal data of third parties into AI prompts. If a task needs that information, keep it in your own systems.

4. Where data lives

Account, business and commerce data is stored in a Supabase project: a Postgres database with row-level security, object storage for images, and serverless functions for privileged operations. Published site files are served from the same source. Payments you initiate are recorded in that database.

Data may be processed in regions where these providers operate. Where a provider offers region selection we use the closest available region, but we cannot guarantee that data never leaves India.

5. Who we share it with

  • Supabase — hosting, authentication, database and storage.
  • Google (Gemini API) — AI generation, receiving only the content needed for the request.
  • GitHub and Vercel — only when you connect them and push or deploy.
  • A domain registrar — only when you order or connect a domain, and only the details the registration requires.
  • A payment provider — when one is enabled and you choose that method.
  • Authorities — where a valid legal request requires disclosure.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. How long we keep it

Account and business data are kept while your account is active. When you request deletion, your profile, business profile, projects, versions, conversations, campaigns and store records are deleted, and payment records are retained in a minimal form where tax or accounting rules require it.

Support tickets are kept for two years so that repeated problems can be recognised. Activity logs are kept for one year.

7. Your rights

You can, from Settings:

  • export your profile, business profile, projects, products and orders as a JSON file;
  • correct your name, phone and business details at any time;
  • delete individual projects, products, customers and conversations;
  • request account deletion, which is logged for review and confirmed before anything is removed.

If you believe data is wrong or held without basis, write to official.shinzora@proton.me and we will respond in writing.

8. Cookies and local storage

We use the minimum needed to keep you signed in: a Supabase session token in browser storage, plus a small preference entry for notification choices and the install prompt dismissal. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels. Clearing browser storage signs you out.

9. Security

Row-level security restricts every table to its owner, with administrator access enforced server-side. Privileged operations run in serverless functions that verify your session on each call. Payment confirmation requires an administrator or a signed provider webhook; the browser has no write path to a verified payment. Provider tokens are stored in a table no browser role can read.

No system is perfect. If you find a vulnerability, tell us at official.shinzora@proton.me and give us a reasonable chance to fix it before disclosing it publicly.

10. Children

Shinzora is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.

11. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we announce it in the dashboard before the change takes effect and update the date at the top of this page.
