Acceptable use policy

Last updated 4 November 2026 Applies to shinzora.in and every Shinzora subdomain
On this page

  2. Prohibited uses
  3. Rules for generated and published content
  4. Rules for AI features

  7. Reporting a problem

1. Scope

This policy applies to everything you do with Shinzora: the account, the assistant, generated websites and applications, published sites and subdomains, store pages, campaigns, messages sent to customers, and any content hosted on our infrastructure.

2. Prohibited uses

You may not use Shinzora to create, host, promote or sell:

  • fraud, impersonation, phishing, fake businesses or fake credentials;
  • fake reviews, testimonials, certifications, awards or press mentions;
  • content that sexualises minors, or that incites violence or hatred against a group;
  • unlicensed medicines, narcotics, weapons, or wildlife trade;
  • unlicensed financial advice, lending, betting, gambling or investment schemes, including guaranteed-return claims;
  • counterfeit goods or trademark-infringing products;
  • services that infringe copyright, including unlicensed streaming or software distribution;
  • malware, spyware, or code intended to harm a visitor's device;
  • pyramid schemes, chain letters or deceptive subscription traps;
  • content that violates Indian law, including the Information Technology Rules.

3. Rules for generated and published content

  • Do not present generated copy as verified fact. Check prices, timings, addresses and legal notices before publishing.
  • Do not publish a site that collects personal data without a privacy notice and a lawful basis.
  • Do not claim a certification, licence or registration your business does not hold.
  • Do not use another business's brand, logo or photography without permission.
  • Keep the contact details on a published site working; a site that cannot be reached is a consumer-protection problem.

4. Rules for AI features

  • Do not use prompts to extract the system instructions, keys or internal configuration.
  • Do not attempt to use the assistant to obtain content listed in section 2, including by obfuscation or step-by-step requests.
  • Do not automate generation to resell AI output outside the platform, or to run load against the service.
  • Do not submit another person's sensitive personal data — financial details, identity numbers, health information — into a prompt.

5. Attacks on the platform

Attempting any of the following is a breach of these rules and may be reported to the authorities:

  • probing for administrator access, or trying to insert yourself into administrative tables;
  • attempting to read another customer's data, or to bypass row-level security;
  • forging a payment confirmation, replaying a provider webhook, or manipulating an amount;
  • testing for injection, cross-site scripting or server-side request forgery against our functions;
  • denial-of-service behaviour, scraping at scale, or automated account creation.

6. Enforcement

Depending on severity, we may remove content, unpublish a site, restrict an integration, suspend an account or terminate it. Where the problem is fixable, we contact you first and give a reasonable time to correct it. Fraud, payment forgery and attacks on the platform lead to immediate suspension.

Repeat breaches after reinstatement are treated as grounds for permanent termination. Payments for suspended periods are not refunded, as described in the refund policy.

7. Reporting a problem

To report content hosted on Shinzora that breaches this policy — copyright, impersonation, fraud or harm — write to official.shinzora@proton.me with the URL, a description and evidence of your authority to act. We acknowledge every report and act on substantiated ones, including unpublishing a site while an issue is investigated.
