Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated 4 November 2026 Applies to shinzora.in and every Shinzora subdomain

1. Principles

We refund when you paid for something you did not receive, or when the platform failed in a way that made the paid feature unusable and we could not fix it. We do not refund work that has already been delivered and used. Every payment in Billing carries a verifiable reference, so any decision is made against a record rather than a recollection.

2. Subscriptions

  • First payment. If you upgrade and change your mind within 7 days, and you have used fewer than 20 AI generations on that plan, we refund the full amount.
  • After 7 days. Subscription periods are not refunded pro rata. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Cancellation. You can cancel at any time from Billing or Settings. Cancellation stops the next renewal; it does not end the current period early and does not trigger an automatic refund.
  • Annual plans. Within the first 14 days and with fewer than 50 generations used, we refund the unused portion minus one month at the monthly rate.

3. One-off services

Managed services — website setup, brand kits, store setup — are billed after a payment is verified. If we have not started the work, the amount is refundable in full. Once work has started, we refund what has not been delivered, assessed against the deliverables listed on the service request. If we cannot deliver at all, you receive a full refund.

4. Domains

Domain registrations are purchased from a registrar and are governed by their terms. Registrations and renewals are generally non-refundable once submitted to the registrar, including when a domain later turns out to be unavailable, because the registrar's own policy applies. Where a registrar fee is refunded to us, we pass it on.

Shinzora does not add a margin to registrar fees; the amount you see is the registrar's price at checkout.

5. Failed, duplicate and unverified payments

  • Unverified payments. A payment that stays unverified is not treated as received. If money left your account without activation, submit the UTR again or contact support — it is either matched or refunded.
  • Duplicate payments. Verified twice for the same purpose: the extra amount is refunded in full.
  • Wrong amount. If you paid less than the required amount, activation waits until the balance is settled. If you paid more, the difference is refunded.
  • Provider failure. If a provider reports a failed payment, no credit is recorded and no refund is due from us; your bank reverses the hold under its own timelines.

6. How to request a refund

  1. Open Billing and copy the payment reference.
  2. Write to official.shinzora@proton.me with the reference, the amount, the date and a one-line reason.
  3. You receive a written decision. If approved, the payment status in Billing changes and the refund is initiated to the original UPI account or payment method.

Refund decisions are made against the record of what was delivered: generations used, versions created, services completed and correspondence. We do not decide from a support chat alone.

7. Timing and method

We aim to decide within 3 working days. Approved UPI refunds typically reach you within 5–7 working days depending on your bank; provider refunds follow the provider's timeline. Refunds go to the original payment method — we do not send money to a different account.

8. What is not refundable

  • AI generations already consumed, beyond the limits described above.
  • Services delivered and accepted, where the deliverables were provided as described.
  • Registrar fees for domains that were registered successfully.
  • Losses caused by content you published, or by a third-party provider outage.
  • Accounts suspended for abuse, fraud or a breach of the acceptable use policy.
